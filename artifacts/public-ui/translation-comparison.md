Người học chỉ cần có khả năng sử dụng trình duyệt, bảng tính và công cụ trình bày cơ bản.<br><br>## Lưu ý<br><br>Đây là khóa học kiến thức và phát triển sản phẩm, **không phải khóa học đầu tư, giao dịch hoặc tư vấn tài chính**.<br><br>Khóa học không yêu cầu người học mua tài sản số, sử dụng tiền thật, phát hành token hoặc thực hiện bất kỳ giao dịch tài chính nào. | **Digital Asset Market** is a course developed by Corelia Academy to help learners build a comprehensive foundation in digital assets from **market, technology and product** perspectives.<br><br>Rather than teaching investing or price prediction, the course focuses on these questions:<br><br>* What are digital assets, and what value do they represent?<br>* Which components does a digital asset transaction pass through?<br>* How do exchanges, wallets, custody services and stablecoins work?<br>* When does a product actually need a blockchain or token?<br>* How can you assess a market opportunity instead of simply following trends?<br>* Which legal, security and user experience considerations should be addressed from the start?<br><br>Through practical examples, ecosystem maps and applied exercises, learners progress from **understanding concepts** to **analyzing markets, identifying problems and developing a viable product direction**.<br><br>## Who is this course for?<br><br>This course is suitable for:<br><br>* Students and beginners exploring digital assets.<br>* Developers who want to understand the market context before building a product.<br>* Product designers, product managers and founders researching new applications.<br>* Business, marketing, growth and community professionals in technology.<br>* Researchers building a systematic foundation for analyzing digital asset markets.<br>* Anyone who wants to understand Web3 and digital assets without starting with trading.<br><br>No blockchain, programming or finance experience is required. Learners only need basic browser, spreadsheet and presentation skills.<br><br>## Note<br><br>This course focuses on knowledge and product development. It is **not a course on investing, trading or financial advice**.<br><br>Learners are not required to buy digital assets, use real money, issue tokens or carry out any financial transactions. |
